E. High / Medium / Low findings:
- **High:** The design’s root-of-trust boundary (GenesisTrustAssumption) remains an irreducible out-of-band assumption. While V8–V17 correctly bound and disclose it, no in-system proof can eliminate the real-world independence assumption of the genesis notaries. - **Medium:** The design relies on an extensive set of root-governed registries and independent authorities. While independence proofs are specified, the complexity increases the risk of misconfiguration or accidental authority overlap. The joint-independence checks (NRVA/RPAA, CAA/root, predicate auditors) are robust but must be mechanically enforced at every relevant commit boundary.
- **Low:** Several falsification cases are duplicative in the enforcement path they exercise (e.g., WDPC-47/72, WDPC-53/68, WDPC-240/244). F. Threshold atomicity/ledger assessment: Adequate. V17-C02 removes the detached recheck fallback and mandates a single atomic operation that revalidates the qualification snapshot, writes the threshold record, enforces durable uniqueness, advances the threshold sequence, and commits all effects together or none. V17-C03 specifies a durable `ReviewThresholdConsumptionLedger` with idempotency keys. This closes the recheck-to-commit race and prevents divergence under crash/partial commit.

G. Cross-gate reuse assessment: Adequate. V17-C04 sets default-deny cross-gate reuse (`CROSS_GATE_REUSE_DENIED`) and requires an exact allow rule binding evidence class/scope, source/destination gates, policy versions, compatibility, independence, maximum reuse count, reason, expiry, and approval. Unauthorized reuse emits `REVIEW_THRESHOLD_REUSE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. The policy is root-governed, preventing self-grant or retroactive legalization.

H. NRVA/RPAA joint-independence assessment: Adequate. V17-C05 requires a root-governed `NormativeReExpressionVerifierRegistry` and current `NRVAIndependenceRecord` plus `NRVA_RPAA_JointIndependenceProof`. The proof evaluates credential, recovery, identity, HSM/KMS/cloud-root, alias, beneficial-owner, super-admin, root-threshold-capable, delegated, and emergency control paths. Conflicting undeclared control evidence emits `NRVA_RPAA_JOINT_INDEPENDENCE_REJECTED`. V17-C06 binds NRVA independence into the exact qualification snapshot and revalidates it inside the atomic threshold transaction.

I. Canonical conflict/corpus assessment: Adequate. V17-C07 specifies that conflicting active evidence classifying the same clause pair both `DISTINCT_OBLIGATIONS` and `SEMANTICALLY_EQUIVALENT` yields `CANONICAL_SEMANTIC_CONFLICT` with no permissive precedence; qualification becomes `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` until a governed new corpus/algorithm proof generation is produced. V17-C08 governs `CanonicalSemanticCorpusRegistry` with digest, fixtures, provenance, independent audit, activation/sunset, freshness, and contamination/retraction state. Stale or compromised corpus emits `CANONICAL_SEMANTIC_CORPUS_STALE`. This prevents permanent overblocking by requiring governed recovery and prevents a compromised corpus from creating false-positive or false-negative semantic decisions without detection.

J. Endpoint-export replay assessment: Adequate. V17-C09 requires exact candidate-commit binding for authoritative endpoint exports. Identical tree/schema/registry/tuple digests do not authorize replay to another commit. Wrong-commit replay emits `REVIEW_ENDPOINT_EXPORT_REPLAY_REJECTED`. This closes the replay bypass.

K. Packet-generation anti-fork assessment: Adequate. V17-C10 specifies a root-governed append-only `PacketGenerationLedger`. Only lineage genesis may have no predecessor. Later generations must bind the current predecessor and advance monotonically. Null-predecessor resets, sibling forks, reused generation numbers, or hidden alternate lineages emit `REVIEW_PACKET_GENERATION_FORK_REJECTED`. This prevents reset, fork, generation reuse, and hidden alternate lineage.

L. Proof-view assessment: Adequate. V17-C11 requires the reviewer-safe proof view to expose atomic threshold commit, consumption-ledger currentness, cross-gate reuse, NRVA registry/joint independence, canonical conflict/corpus currentness, endpoint exact-commit binding, and packet anti-fork state.
