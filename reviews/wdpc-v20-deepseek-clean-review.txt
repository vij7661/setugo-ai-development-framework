3. Overall disposition: `CHANGES_REQUIRED`

No V20 falsification case has been executed. V20 is directionally additive and generally narrows V19 in the fail-closed direction, but it still leaves several authority/evaluator/self-grant gaps and some non-deterministic expected endpoints.

4. Critical / High / Medium / Low findings

Critical
- V20-C04 reconciliation lacks an explicit independent reconciliation evaluator/authority. The same caller/actor that caused `REVIEW_THRESHOLD_COMMIT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` can apparently evaluate the deterministic table. Even with a fixed table, if the evaluator is not independently bound and the evidence inputs are not independently authenticated, the actor can choose `NO_COMMIT_CONFIRMED` and retry with the same idempotency key, creating a self-grant/double-count path.
- V20-C02 “same governed object class” does not prevent object-class redefinition, aliasing, splitting, or renaming so that the same underlying ledger is moved into a new class with a lower threshold. This can bypass absolute threshold non-weakening without an explicit threshold-lowering policy.

High
- V20-C05 introduces an independent `DependencyUniverseAuthority`, but no endpoint, independence record, or falsification case clearly prevents that authority from being controlled by the candidate, corpus/algorithm producer, beneficiary, or a root-threshold-capable subset. WDPC-311 only covers circular self-derivation by the corpus/algorithm under change.
- V20-C07 defines exact witness quorum/currentness/key lifecycle, but does not clearly bind the witness quorum policy itself to V20-C02 anti-weakening. A witness quorum could be prospectively weakened unless the witness policy class is explicitly covered.
- V20-C03 makes authority-bearing publication classification root-governed, but there is no case for sub-threshold mutation, local override, or self-classification of the `AuthorityPublicationClassRegistry`.
- V20-C09 execution-attestation issuer binding says the implementation under test cannot self-grant unless policy permits it. There is no anti-weakening control on the attestation-issuer policy itself, so policy could be broadened to permit self-attestation.
- V20-C10 secret-safe proof views depend on an approved non-reversible fingerprint scheme, but entropy/salt/reversibility criteria are not specified. Low-entropy credentials may remain recoverable from fingerprints.

Medium
- WDPC-310, WDPC-311, and WDPC-318 use “or” between expected endpoints. Deterministic falsifiability is weakened unless the condition selecting each endpoint is made exact and phase-bound.
- Missing positive controls for: independent reconciliation evaluator; correct authority-publication classification; governed conflict resolution of conflicting independent-support exemptions; witness-quorum anti-weakening.
- V20-C11 exact activation scope semantics depends on an “active activation-schema policy.” That policy is not visibly subject to anti-weakening or root-governed scope-schema integrity.
- V20-C12 proof-view additions omit explicit fields for object-class identity/non-redefinition, reconciliation evaluator independence, witness-quorum policy version/anti-weakening, and activation-schema policy integrity.

Low
- V20-C14 adds only two new endpoints; several new controls do not have dedicated failure endpoints, e.g. dependency-universe authority independence failure, witness-quorum weakening, activation-schema weakening.
- The V20 coverage map is useful but does not yet show unique-enforcement-path accounting for the new V20 controls.

8. Explicit false-green path tests

- Absolute threshold non-weakening:  
  False-green still possible via object-class redefinition, ledger renaming, class splitting, or alias creation that places the same governed ledger under a new class with a lower threshold. V20-C02 only protects “same governed object class.”

- Authority-publication classification:  
  False-green possible if local policy or implementation labels an authority-bearing publication as non-authority-bearing, or if the root registry is mutated/overridden without sub-threshold protection. Runtime may also fail to consult the registry before treating publication as optional.

- Reconciliation precedence:  
  False-green possible if the same caller evaluates `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`; if duplicate identical commits exist; if publication is omitted from mandatory evidence; if a stale/unavailable witness is treated as consistent; or if signed evidence is not independently authenticated.

- Independent dependency-universe derivation:  
  False-green possible if the corpus/algorithm under change defines or filters its own eligible set, if the `DependencyUniverseAuthority` is controlled by candidate/beneficiary, or if its independence proof is missing.

- Support-exemption freshness/conflicts:  
  False-green possible via expired/revoked exemption reuse, conflicting exemptions, self-support through a different principal, or absence of an independent conflict-resolution authority.

- Witness quorum/currentness/key lifecycle:  
  False-green possible via witness-quorum policy weakening, key rotation without governed continuity, revoked-key checkpoint replay, lag-bound bypass, or control/admin/recovery-domain overlap hidden behind nominal identity separation.

- Execution-attestation replay/issuer binding:  
  False-green possible via replay across action instance, candidate, control version, or runtime path; via self-declared issuer; or via policy broadening of allowed issuer class.

- Secret-safe proof views:  
  False-green possible if fingerprints are reversible, low-entropy, unsalted, or if raw secret material appears in allowed metadata fields.

- Activation scope semantics:  
  False-green possible via alias, wildcard, parent-resource expansion, derived resource names, or activation-schema policy broadening.

9. Remaining caller/evaluator/self-grant path

- Reconciliation evaluator: the commit initiator may also evaluate `REVIEW_THRESHOLD_COMMIT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` and choose `NO_COMMIT_CONFIRMED`.
- Authority-publication classification: local or root-policy misclassification can exclude publication from the atomic bound set.
- Dependency-universe authority: no clear independence record/failure endpoint prevents candidate/beneficiary control.
- Witness-quorum policy: no anti-weakening rule prevents prospective quorum weakening.
- Execution-attestation issuer policy: no anti-weakening rule prevents broadening who may issue qualifying attestations.
- Activation-schema policy: no anti-weakening rule prevents broadening exact activation scope semantics.
- Object-class identity: no anti-redefinition rule prevents creating a new lower-threshold class for the same ledger.

10. Missing negative/positive cases

Missing negative cases
- Sub-threshold mutation of `AuthorityPublicationClassRegistry`.
- Object-class redefinition/renaming/splitting to evade root-threshold non-weakening.
- Reconciliation evaluator self-grant or collusion with commit initiator.
- Dependency-universe authority control-domain overlap with candidate/beneficiary/root-threshold-capable subset.
- Witness-quorum policy weakening.
- Execution-attestation issuer-policy weakening.
- Activation-schema policy weakening/broadening.
- Reversible or low-entropy credential fingerprint in secret-safe proof.

Missing positive cases
- Correct authority-publication classification and atomic inclusion under a valid registry.
- Independent reconciliation evaluator correctly selects each terminal branch.
- Governed conflict resolution of conflicting independent-support exemptions.
- Witness-quorum policy same-or-stronger rotation.
- Object-class identity preservation across root-policy rotations.
- Attestation-issuer policy same-or-stronger rotation.
- Activation-schema policy same-or-stronger rotation.

11. Regression/precedence analysis against inherited V5–V19 controls

V20 is additive and appears to narrow V19 in the fail-closed direction. V20-C02 is stricter than V19-C04 by rejecting threshold-lowering at the policy-change boundary. V20-C04 is stricter than V19-C03 by fixing a deterministic reconciliation precedence table. V20-C05 adds an independent dependency-universe requirement. V20-C06 tightens exemption freshness/conflicts. V20-C07 tightens witness quorum/currentness/key lifecycle. V20-C09–C11 add anti-replay, secret-safe proof, and exact activation-scope controls.

No explicit weakening of V5–V19 controls is evident. However, the identified gaps are not covered by inherited controls and therefore remain live regression risks: object-class redefinition, reconciliation evaluator self-grant, witness-quorum weakening, attestation-issuer policy weakening, activation-schema policy weakening, and dependency-universe authority independence.
