## Classification

**TEST/RUNNER ASSERTION DEFECT — not a KMS signing mechanism failure.**

The runner used:

`sign1.get("KeyId") == md.get("KeyId")`

AWS returned different documented identifier forms on the two successful APIs in this execution:

- `DescribeKey.KeyMetadata.KeyId`: key UUID `57d95d4f-9b80-44e4-badf-0330ba9f897c`
- `Sign.KeyId`: full key ARN `arn:aws:kms:ap-southeast-2:297165774800:key/57d95d4f-9b80-44e4-badf-0330ba9f897c`

Thus the boolean endpoint failed even though the signature was generated by the exact frozen KMS key and independently verified.
