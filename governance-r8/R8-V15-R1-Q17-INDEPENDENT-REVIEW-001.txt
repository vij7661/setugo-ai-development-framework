**M-01**
2. MEDIUM
3. `tools/verify_pinned_governance_actions.py::verify`
4. The `uses:` matcher requires the line to end immediately after `\S+`. A mutable action reference followed by a trailing comment (`uses: actions/checkout@v4 # comment`) is ignored. If another pinned action exists in the same workflow, `uses` remains non-empty and the verifier passes, leaving the mutable action unpinned.
